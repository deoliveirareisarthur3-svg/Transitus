TRANSITUS

A game of strategy and movement

← Back to Home
White's turn
Move a piece one square forward or sideways.
Full turns: 0 / 120

Rules

  • Each player starts with 8 pieces on an 8×8 board.
  • White moves toward the top; Black moves toward the bottom.
  • Move one square forward or sideways. No diagonal or backward moves.
  • Pieces cannot jump over or capture other pieces.
  • Reach the opposite end of the board to win.
  • If a player has no legal moves, the last player to move loses.
  • If a position occurs three times, the player who started the repetition loses.
  • If 120 full turns pass without another result, the game is a draw.
